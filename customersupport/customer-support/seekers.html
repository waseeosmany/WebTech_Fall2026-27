Getting started

1. Create your account

Register with your email and verify it using the link we send.

2. Complete your profile

Add your name, contact number, location and a short summary about yourself.

3. Upload your CV

Use a PDF or Word file. Check that your details are correct before saving.

4. Add skills and experience

List the skills employers search for, such as the tools and languages you use.

5. Search and filter jobs

Filter by role, location and skills to find jobs that fit you.

6. Apply and track

Press Apply, then watch the status in My Applications: Pending, Shortlisted or Rejected.

Having trouble with a step? Send us a New Request.
