For Job Seekers

How do I create a profile?
Register with your email, verify it, then fill in your name, contact details, skills and experience. A complete profile gets noticed more often by employers.
How do I apply for a job?
Open the job, read the requirements and press Apply. Check that your CV is up to date first, because the employer sees what you submit.
Why can't I upload my CV?
Check that the file is in a supported format (PDF or Word) and try a smaller file. If it still fails, send us a request with a screenshot of the error.
How do I know my application status?
Open My Applications in your account. The status changes to Pending, Shortlisted or Rejected as the employer reviews it.

For Employers

How do I post a job?
Log in to your employer account, choose Post a Job, fill in the title, requirements and deadline, then publish.
How do I see who applied?
Open your posting and choose Applicants. You can sort them by match score and filter by status.
How do I shortlist or reject a candidate?
Open the candidate's profile and use the Shortlist or Reject button. The candidate's status updates straight away.
How do I edit or close a posting?
Open the posting from your dashboard. Edit the details, or close it when you have found the right person.
