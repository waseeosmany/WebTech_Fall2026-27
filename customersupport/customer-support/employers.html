Posting your first job

  • Step 1: Log in to your employer account.
  • Step 2: Choose Post a Job and fill in the title, requirements and deadline.
  • Step 3: Publish the job and share the link if you like.
  • Step 4: Open Applicants to sort by match score and filter by status.
  • Step 5: Use Shortlist or Reject on each candidate profile.
